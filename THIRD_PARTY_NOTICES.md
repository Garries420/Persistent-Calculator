## Frankfurter

Persistent Calculator obtains reference currency metadata and exchange rates from the public Frankfurter API:

- Website: https://frankfurter.dev/
- API: https://api.frankfurter.dev/v2/

Frankfurter data is used at runtime and is not presented as a Microsoft currency feed. Cached responses remain in the user's Documents folder.
